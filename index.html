Click a highlighted vertex to place a hexagon.

Pick a polygon in the toolbar, then click a highlighted vertex on the boundary to place it there. In Delete mode, click a boundary vertex to remove its cell; only cells with nothing built on them can be removed.

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

Solver substeps per frame (more = stiffer and slower).
Constraint passes per substep (more = rules agree better, but slower).
Resistance to swerve: bending around the strip's thickness axis, within its own plane. 1 = none allowed.
Resistance to twisting along a strip.
Resistance to curl: bending around the strip's width axis, out of its flat plane. 1 = rigid between neighbouring nodes; gentle curls are still only slowly resisted.
Coulomb friction where crossing strips are pressed together.
Static grip at ordinary crossings while touching, regardless of pressure. Holds hole sizes, but strips start curving sideways.
How firmly strips are clipped together at apex crossings (1 = pinned: no slip at all).
Fraction of velocity removed per frame (high = settles calmly).
In-surface width of each woven strip (edge length = 1).
Strip thickness; also the over/under separation at crossings.
