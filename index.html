Weave graph designer

Click a highlighted vertex to place a hexagon.

Pick a polygon in the toolbar, then click a highlighted vertex on the boundary to place it there. In Delete mode, click a boundary vertex to remove its cell; only cells with nothing built on them can be removed.

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
